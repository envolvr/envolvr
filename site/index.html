Pricing

Two ways to pay. One of them is cheaper.

PAY AS YOU GO

USDG, per request

Sign in with your wallet, deposit USDG and call the endpoint. No token, no lockup.

  • Suppliers' list prices, 5% on deposit
  • Billed to the micro-dollar, on the receipt
  • No token exposure
Deposit USDG
STAKE

A daily compute allowance

Stake NVLR and draw your share of the daily inference pool, refreshed at 00:00 UTC. Prepaid access to compute, not a claim on revenue.

  • More stake, more daily inference
  • Drawn before your USDG balance
  • Unstake with a cooldown
Stake NVLR

ⓘ Next: an open provider network. Providers stake NVLR to serve, and a failed attestation or a wrong model is proven by the receipt itself and slashed.
