Reasoning leaks

An agent that thinks with a hosted model hands that provider its positions, its signals and its intent. On a transparent chain, the surrounding job and payment data links the two together.

Privacy you cannot check

Providers say they keep no logs. There is no way to confirm it. The default mode of every consumer private AI product is a contract and a promise.

No paper trail

Regulated finance has to show which model at which version produced a decision, without exposing the strategy behind it. Nothing shipping today does both.

02

Four steps, and no trusted middleman.

  1. 01

    Verify the enclave

    The client pulls the attestation quote and checks the measurement against a known build. If it does not match, the prompt never leaves the machine.

  2. 02

    Encrypt to the enclave

    The prompt is encrypted to a key held only inside the attested gateway enclave. It is read only inside attested enclaves, the gateway's and the provider's, so nothing outside them sees plaintext. That includes us.

  3. 03

    Run attested inference

    Inference happens on a GPU TEE that the gateway verifies before it forwards a single byte. The operator cannot read the prompt or the response, and the receipt names the model and the attested session that served it.

  4. 04

    Return a signed receipt

    The enclave signs a receipt that binds the model, the attested provider session, commitments to the exact request and response, and what was billed. Its hash is anchored on Robinhood Chain within ten minutes.

03

The receipt is the product.

Privacy covers one half of the claim. The other half is proving which model, served by which attested enclave, produced a specific output, and what it cost, checkable by anyone, without trusting envolvr or the provider. The proof is rooted in the silicon itself: Intel TDX and NVIDIA Confidential Computing.

The verifier is open source and deterministic. Run it yourself, or hand it to your auditor. Receipt hashes are anchored on Robinhood Chain in an append-only log, so a provider's entire history stays auditable.

  • Attestation quote validation
  • Model and provider binding
  • Prompt commitment for selective disclosure
  • Billing record, recomputable to the micro-dollar
  • On-chain anchoring and per-provider history

05

Four things the receipt proves.

  1. 01

    The enclave is genuine and runs a measured, specific build.

    Attestation binds the quote to a known-good measurement. A different binary cannot produce a valid receipt.

  2. 02

    The GPU operator cannot read your prompt or your response.

    Your client encrypts to the enclave's public key. The operator holds the hardware, not the key.

  3. 03

    Every response is bound to its model and its attested provider.

    The signed receipt names the model and cites the attested provider session that served it. Change either and verification fails.

  4. 04

    You can verify all of the above without trusting us.

    The verifier is open source and deterministic, and every receipt hash is anchored on Robinhood Chain.

Open verifier · deterministic · anchored on Robinhood Chain

06

What the network is made of.

  1. L1

    Client

    The SDK and the verifying proxy check the gateway's attestation before anything is sent, then speak only to a key held inside the attested enclave. Every receipt is kept, ready to verify.

    attestation check · pinned channel
  2. L2

    Gateway

    Routes jobs to verified providers from inside a TDX enclave. Its code and configuration are measured and public, its keys never leave it, and envolvr's operators cannot read what passes through.

    TEE-hosted · measured · no operator access
  3. L3

    Compute

    GPU TEE providers serve open-weight models on confidential hardware, verified for every request. The operator holds the machine, not the data. Next: attested weights, the exact weights measured inside the enclave.

    NVIDIA CC · TDX · verified per request
  4. L4

    Verifier

    Open source and deterministic. It validates the quote, the keyset, the provider session, the request and response commitments, the billing record and the on-chain anchor, and it can be run by anyone.

    open source · reproducible
  5. L5

    Registry

    Next: providers stake NVLR to serve. A failed attestation or a wrong model is proven by the receipt itself, downtime by public challenges. Nothing is slashed for something we cannot detect.

    next · stake · objective slashing
  6. L6

    Settlement

    Jobs settle in USDG and every receipt hash is anchored on Robinhood Chain in an append-only log, so a provider's full history stays auditable by anyone.

    USDG · Robinhood Chain
