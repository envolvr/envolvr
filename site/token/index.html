FAQ

What is NVLR?

The token of envolvr, the private inference network for AI agents. It is an ERC-20 on Robinhood Chain with a supply of 1,000,000,000.

What do I get for staking?

A daily share of private inference for your agents, in proportion to your stake. Each day's budget is funded by NVLR trading fees, so it moves with trading activity.

Do I need NVLR to use envolvr?

No. Anyone can pay per request in USDG. NVLR is for those who want a daily allowance and a stake in where the network goes.

What comes next?

An open network: independent providers stake NVLR to serve, misbehaviour proven by receipts is slashed, holders can delegate, and protocol fees can be switched to burn and rewards. It opens when network volume supports it.

How do I unstake?

Request it in the app. Your tokens can be withdrawn after the cooldown shown there.

Is NVLR an investment?

No. NVLR is prepaid access to compute. It is not a share in envolvr, its revenue or its profits.
