The daily budget

Trading NVLR funds the compute that NVLR stakers use. The more NVLR trades, the more private inference stakers share.

Every tradeNVLR trading fees

Fees from NVLR trading go to the protocol.

→
Before each dayThe day's budget, on chain

The budget is set from those fees and written to the staking contract before the day starts.

→
At 00:00 UTCSplit by stake

Each staker's allowance is the budget times their share of total stake at the day's start.

→
All dayPrivate inference

Your agents spend it in attested enclaves. Unused allowance does not roll over.

ⓘ The budget varies day to day with trading activity. Anyone can read past and upcoming budgets from the staking contract.

Today envolvr operates the network. Next, it opens to independent providers, and NVLR becomes their security bond.

FAQ

Do I need NVLR to use envolvr?

No. You can deposit USDG and pay per request. NVLR is for people who prefer a daily allowance.

How is my allowance calculated?

At 00:00 UTC each day, your allowance is that day's budget times your stake divided by total stake at that moment. It is computed on chain by the staking contract, so anyone can check it.

Where does the daily budget come from?

From NVLR trading fees. Before each day starts, the budget is set from those fees and written to the staking contract, so it moves with trading activity.

When does new stake start counting?

From the next 00:00 UTC. Stake added during a day counts for the following day's allowance.

Does unused allowance roll over?

No. Each day starts fresh. Any request beyond your allowance is paid from your USDG balance, if you have one.

How do I unstake?

Request an unstake in the app. That stake stops counting from the next day, and the tokens can be withdrawn after the cooldown shown in the app.

Which models can the allowance pay for?

Every model envolvr serves, at the same list prices as USDG requests. See Models.

Is NVLR an investment?

No. NVLR is prepaid access to compute. It is not a share in envolvr, its revenue or its profits.
